Information sources
* info/Wallentin.pdf - Primary study publication
* info/Mahaffey.pdf - Publication assessing regional heterogeneity in PLATO

the MRCT_manuscript.tex document. We want to explore the treatment effect in the USA region versus the
rest of the world using this methodology on the synthetic data.

- sample size per arm (Table 1 in Wallentin.pdf)
- Cumulative incidence rate over time per treatment arm (as in Figure 1 in Wallentin.pdf)
- hazard ratio of the primary analysis (Caption of Figure 1 in Wallentin.pdf)
- follow-up time/censoring distribution for primary analysis
- sample size per arm within region (US vs ROW) and maintenance aspirin dose group (Figure 3 in Mahaffey.pdf)
- hazard ratio for the analyses within region (US vs ROW) and maintenance aspirin dose group (Figure 3 in Mahaffey.pdf)
- In addition to maintenance aspirin dose, include further covariates but only those that are both in Table 1 and Figure 2A of Mahaffey.pdf,
  make sure the distribution across US and ROW stays intact as in Table 1
- The synthetic data should approximately reproduce approximately the univariate interaction analyses in Figure 2A im Mahaffey.pdf

Please provide R code to create the synthetic data and the data in csv format.

Also provide an R file that compares the summaries extracted from the information sources and the generated synthetic data.
